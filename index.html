Privacy Policy

At NovaRX, your privacy is our highest priority. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our platform.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, phone number, and health-related data when you place orders or consult doctors. We also collect usage data automatically, including your IP address, browser type, and pages visited.

  • Account registration details (name, email, phone)
  • Order history and prescription uploads
  • Device and browser information
  • Location data (only when delivery is requested)

2. How we use your information

We use your information to process orders, provide customer support, personalize your experience, and send important updates about your orders. We never sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data security

All data is encrypted in transit using TLS and at rest using AES-256 encryption. We follow industry-standard security practices and conduct regular audits to protect your information.

4. Prescription data

Prescription documents you upload are handled with the highest level of care, accessible only to licensed pharmacists processing your order. They are never shared or used for marketing purposes.

5. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. You can manage your data preferences in your account settings or contact us at privacy@novarx.com.

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and remember your preferences. You can disable non-essential cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect some features.

7. Contact us

For any privacy-related questions, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@novarx.com or write to us at NovaRX HQ, 123 Health Street, Mumbai 400001.
